To configure a product to automatically generate Manufacturing Requests
from procurements instead of directly create manufacturing orders you
need to:

1.  Go to the products that you want them to trigger manufacturing
    requests.
2.  Go to the *Inventory* tab.
3.  Check the box of a *manufacture* route and the box of *Manufacturing
    Request*.

Alternatively, you can enable the *Manufacturing Request* checkbox on a
warehouse route (*Inventory \> Configuration \> Routes*). Every
procurement resolved through a *Manufacture* or *Buy* rule of that route
will generate a Manufacturing Request instead of a manufacturing or
purchase order.
